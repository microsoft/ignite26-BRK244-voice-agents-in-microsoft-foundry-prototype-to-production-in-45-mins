## Current delivery status

Notebooks 01–03 and shared session helpers are implemented for the GA realtime

the repository does not include evidence of an authenticated live run.

| Order | Guidance | Exercise |
|-------|----------|----------|

Allow about 5 minutes for setup checks, 8 for quickstart, 10 for turn-taking,
and 10 for multilingual comparisons, after obtaining access in advance.
The intended 45-minute sequence continues with a focused tracing and evaluation
segment in a later increment. Tools, routing, pronunciation, human escalation,
API workflows, recovery, and supported multimodal interaction are future optional
modules, not implemented exercises.

## Participation

During a guided session, follow the presenter through the table in order.
On your own, complete setup first and record your observations for each exercise.
Use neutral synthetic utterances only: do not upload customer data, real support
conversations, credentials, or personal information.

Live Azure execution requires an authorized deployment and may incur charges.
Offline checks cannot establish service availability, speech quality, latency,
or successful interruption.
